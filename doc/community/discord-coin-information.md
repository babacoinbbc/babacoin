🟡 **BABACOIN | BBC**

Welcome to Babacoin — a Proof-of-Work cryptocurrency on the existing BBC mainnet, with a desktop wallet and Smartnodes.

⚙️ **NETWORK AT A GLANCE**
• **Coin:** Babacoin
• **Ticker:** BBC
• **Consensus:** Proof of Work
• **Algorithm:** GhostRider
• **Target block time:** approximately 120 seconds

💻 **THE V2 WALLET EXPERIENCE**
Day and night themes, clearer balances and transactions, visible startup and rescan progress, a guided Windows installer, optional Explorer peer discovery, and Windows, macOS and Linux packages. Check each release's platform support notes for architecture and runtime requirements.

⚡ **BLOCKCHAIN BOOTSTRAP**
Full and incremental bootstrap downloads are available in Releases to reduce block downloading. Your node still performs normal validation.

🔔 **IMPORTANT V2 NETWORK UPGRADE**
All validating wallets, exchanges, pools and nodes must upgrade before the first **2,000,000 BBC Smartnode registration**. Early registration is available in V2.

**1,800,000 BBC collateral stops qualifying for rewards from block 1,250,000.** Installing V2 does not automatically convert collateral or register a Smartnode.
